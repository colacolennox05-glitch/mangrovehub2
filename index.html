Mangrove Ecology & Science

Understand how these intertidal ecosystems mitigate climate risks, build coastal defense, and support rich aquatic biodiversity.

Blue Carbon Storage Powerhouses

Mangrove trees capture vast quantities of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and lock it inside organic-rich anaerobic soils beneath intertidal mudflats.

  • Sequesters carbon 4x faster than inland tropical rainforests.
  • Stores carbon in sediment layer for centuries without rapid breakdown.

Natural Storm & Erosion Barriers

Complex aerial root matrices function as natural bio-shields, absorbing intense energy from ocean storm surges, tsunamis, and high tides.

  • Reduces wave height energy by up to 66% over 100 meters.
  • Traps land silt and prevents coastal shore runoff erosion.

Estuarine Nursery & Biodiversity Safehaven

Over 75% of tropical commercial fish species rely on mangrove root structures for protection during early larval and juvenile growth stages.
